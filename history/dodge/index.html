EDITORIAL ANALYSIS

What the Dodge Story Reveals

A brand history is more useful when it explains why the important decisions mattered.

Dodge developed around a distinctly American combination of utility and performance. Its trucks established a practical identity, while muscle cars and later high-output products made the badge synonymous with accessible performance for several generations.

The brand's history is especially interesting because its product role changed repeatedly inside Chrysler and later Stellantis. The continuity is not a single body style; it is an emphasis on strong visual presence, usable performance and products that give a mainstream portfolio an emotional edge.
