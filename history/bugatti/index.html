BUGATTI · 1909–2026

Bugatti History

From Ettore Bugatti’s Molsheim enterprise to modern hypercars, Bugatti represents an unusually consistent belief that engineering, craftsmanship, aesthetics and extreme performance can belong in the same automobile.

01 · ORIGINS

Where the Story Began

Ettore Bugatti founded his automobile company in Molsheim in 1909 after developing experience with technically adventurous machines. The original brand was never built around mass production. Its identity depended on precision engineering, unusually elegant proportions and competition success. That combination created a reputation that survived the original company’s financial collapse and later made the name valuable enough to be revived decades after production stopped.

02 · TIMELINE

Key Turning Points

1909

Bugatti founded

Ettore Bugatti establishes Automobiles E. Bugatti in Molsheim.

1910

Type 13 development

A compact competition-focused design establishes Bugatti’s engineering direction.

1924

Type 35 debut

The Type 35 begins one of the most successful racing careers in Grand Prix history.

1934

Type 57 introduced

Jean Bugatti’s influence becomes increasingly visible in the company’s grand touring cars.

1939

Jean Bugatti dies

The death of Ettore’s son removes a central creative force from the company.

1947

Ettore Bugatti dies

The original era effectively loses its founding technical authority.

1987

Bugatti revived in Italy

Romano Artioli creates Bugatti Automobili S.p.A.

1991

EB110 arrives

The modern revival launches a technically ambitious four-wheel-drive supercar.

1998

Volkswagen acquires Bugatti

The brand receives the financial scale needed for another reinvention.

2005

Veyron production begins

The Veyron redefines the modern hypercar through extreme power, speed and engineering complexity.

2016

Chiron introduced

Bugatti develops the W16 hypercar concept further.

2021

Rimac-Bugatti partnership

A new ownership structure combines Bugatti heritage with Rimac’s electric-performance expertise.

05 · CORPORATE HISTORY

Ownership and Strategic Change

Bugatti’s corporate history is defined by discontinuity. The original Molsheim company was a small specialist manufacturer, while later revivals depended on larger financial structures. Romano Artioli revived the name with the EB110, Volkswagen turned it into a global hypercar symbol, and the modern Bugatti organization moved into a partnership model shaped by Rimac technology. The recurring question is the same: how can a tiny-volume manufacturer finance engineering that is far more expensive than its production scale would normally support?

EDITORIAL ANALYSIS

What the Bugatti Story Reveals

A brand history is more useful when it explains why the important decisions mattered.

Bugatti's early history combined advanced engineering, artistic bodywork and extraordinary competition success. The marque became famous for lightweight construction and refined mechanical solutions at a time when racing and road-car development were closely connected.

The modern Bugatti story is different in scale but similar in ambition. Under Volkswagen Group and later Rimac-linked ownership, the brand became a laboratory for extreme performance, complex powertrains and limited-volume craftsmanship. Its historical continuity is best seen in the pursuit of exceptional engineering rather than in any single technical recipe.
