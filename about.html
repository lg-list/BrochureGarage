About Car Brochure Archive

Car Brochure Archive is an independent reference index for automotive brochure PDFs. The site organizes sales brochures, model catalogs, specification sheets, and range guides by manufacturer and model so readers can compare trim language, engines, dimensions, equipment, and market positioning across model years.

The archive is built for shoppers, owners, collectors, researchers, and writers who need a fast way to find original brochure material without digging through scattered manufacturer pages. Each brand page groups brochures by model family, while model pages focus on year-by-year PDF records when documents are available.

How pages are organized

Brand pages summarize the available brochure records for one manufacturer. Model pages narrow the archive to a single model family and list brochure years, titles, file sizes, and download links. The home page is a compact brand directory designed for quick scanning.

Editorial approach

The site adds its own organization, model grouping, research notes, comparison guidance, and page metadata around brochure records. The goal is to help readers understand which brochure to open, what details to compare, and how to treat each PDF as historical reference material rather than current manufacturer advice.

The archive catalog was imported from Auto-Brochures. Catalog titles and sizes are supplied labels. They do not establish that a PDF has been individually inspected. Reviewed documents have a separate verification record, including a file hash, page count, current size and review date.

Our CR-V comparison uses specific pages of two brochures to explain equipment differences. Brochure Finder searches the complete catalog and exports citations. The methodology page documents the distinction between imported metadata and reviewed facts.

Maintenance and corrections

The site is maintained through the BrochureGarage project. Public catalog corrections can be submitted through its issue tracker. Include the document title, URL and supporting evidence. No professional automotive qualification or manufacturer relationship is implied by the project name.

Independence

Car Brochure Archive is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any vehicle manufacturer. Brand names, model names, logos, and brochure titles are used for identification and historical reference.
