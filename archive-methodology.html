Archive Methodology

How the brochure archive is built

Car Brochure Archive is an independent catalog of imported brochure records and reviewed research. The catalog comes from the Auto-Brochures collection. Most entries have not been individually reviewed inside the PDF; their titles, sizes and sections are supplied catalog labels.

What a brochure record contains

Each record preserves the supplied brochure title and file name, displayed file size, source section when available, and the PDF path used by the archive. Those fields create the brand indexes, model pages, year summaries, archive report, and sitemap. An unclear year is left unspecified rather than guessed.

How models are grouped

Model names are normalized for navigation so related records can be found together. This grouping is an editorial convenience, not a claim that every market uses the same name. A brochure may cover a range, body style, trim family, or multiple models; the original title remains visible so readers can judge its scope.

How to use the collection

Use the archive to locate and compare historical sales literature. Check the exact year, market, print wording, and footnotes before treating a detail as evidence. Current pricing, safety ratings, availability, recalls, legal requirements, and purchase decisions require a current manufacturer or government source.

Quality and correction process

Build checks verify local page links, canonical URLs, robots directives and sitemap entries. They do not prove that every remotely hosted PDF opens. Remote downloads are sampled separately. For reviewed files, we record a hash, PDF page count, current byte size and review date in the verification record. Readers can report a title error or broken file through the contact page.

Document rights

Image credits: the historical Oldsmobile logo is from Wikimedia Commons; its source page documents the copyright label. Other brand marks identify catalog categories and do not imply affiliation.

The repository does not currently contain individual redistribution licenses for the imported documents. Public availability and attribution do not establish permission. Manufacturer literature and photographs remain third-party material. The site's comparisons, catalog organization and research tools are separate editorial work. Rights holders can use the contact page to identify a document and request review or removal.

How to cite a record

For a research note, cite the brand and model page together with the exact brochure title and year shown in the record. When a claim depends on a specific page of a PDF, include the PDF title and page number in your own notes. This makes it possible to distinguish a period brochure statement from a current specification or a later owner report.

Updates and collection limits

The collection changes when a file is corrected, a record is added, a link stops working, or a rights request requires a page or document to be changed. The counts shown on the archive report are snapshots of the current metadata, not a claim that the archive contains every brochure published by a manufacturer.

Independence and limitations

Car Brochure Archive is independent and does not imply manufacturer endorsement. Brand names, model names, logos, and document titles are used for identification and research reference. Brochures may differ by country, trim, print date, and model year, so the document itself remains the primary historical source.
